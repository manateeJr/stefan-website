video studio · Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

video studio is a personal, single-user tool run by Stefan Andonov. I use it to edit my own short videos and to send them to my own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. It has no other users and no sign-up.

What it accesses

  • TikTok: my own account's basic profile (display name), and, when I tap to share, uploads of my own videos to my TikTok inbox. Through the TikTok API for Business: my own account's video list and analytics (views, watch time, retention).
  • YouTube: permission to upload my own videos to my own channel.
  • Instagram: publishing my own reels and reading my own reel insights.

Where data is kept

Access tokens and analytics are stored only on my own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions), in your Google Account (Security → Third-party connections) or in Instagram's Apps and Websites settings. Deleting the local token files removes the tool's access immediately.
